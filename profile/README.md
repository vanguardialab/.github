# Vanguardia Lab

### Enterprise technology, business automation and applied AI for Latin America

Vanguardia Lab is a B2B technology company based in Costa Rica. We help organizations implement, integrate and automate the platforms they use to manage sales, customer service, operations and business knowledge.

## What we do

- SaaS implementation and consulting
- Business process automation
- Systems and API integrations
- AI assistants, agents and MCP-based tools
- CRM and omnichannel customer service solutions
- Business intelligence and analytics
- Corporate training and technology adoption

## Technologies we work with

Bitrix24 · Alaio · AWS · OpenAI · Respond.io · Make · Webflow · Moodle · GitHub · Infomaniak

## AI and integration initiatives

We design practical AI solutions that connect business knowledge, operational systems and enterprise workflows.

Our initiatives include:

- Model Context Protocol integrations
- AI-powered knowledge access
- Business process assistants
- Customer service and support automation
- Enterprise AI adoption programs
- Secure integrations between SaaS platforms
